# Help and support

A question, a problem, an idea? Write to us at [{{supportEmail}}](mailto:{{supportEmail}}) and we will answer as soon as we can. The answers to the most common questions are below.

## General

### What is Jeannot?

Jeannot is a garden assistant. It helps you plan and look after your garden with a lunar and biodynamic calendar, local weather forecasts and alerts, a plan of your garden drawn to scale, the follow-up of each plant from sowing to harvest, a plant encyclopedia and an AI assistant that answers your questions and recognises plants and diseases from a photo.

### On which devices can I use Jeannot?

Jeannot is available on iPhone and iPad from the [App Store]({{appStoreUrl}}), on Android from [Google Play]({{playStoreUrl}}), and in any recent browser on a computer with the web version at [{{webAppLabel}}]({{webAppUrl}}).

### Do I need an internet connection?

Yes. Jeannot needs a connection to fetch the weather, keep your garden in sync between your devices and reach the assistant.

### In which languages is Jeannot available?

In English and in French, including the encyclopedia and the assistant. You can change the language in the settings.

## The web version

### What is the web version of Jeannot?

It is Jeannot in your browser, at [{{webAppLabel}}]({{webAppUrl}}), with nothing to install. A large screen and a mouse make it easier to draw the plan of your garden, read the encyclopedia or prepare the season.

### Do I need another account for the web version?

No. Sign in with the same account as in the app: your garden, your plants, your tasks and your subscription follow you.

### Can I subscribe to Premium from the web version?

Not for now. Subscribe in the app on your iPhone, iPad or Android device, then sign in on the web with the same account and Premium is active there too.

## Features

### What is the garden plan?

A map of your garden, drawn to scale. Trace its outline on a satellite view or enter its dimensions, add your beds, greenhouse, paths and trees, then place your plants one by one, in rows or over a whole area. The plan keeps the history of each bed, so Jeannot can advise you on crop rotation and on good and bad neighbours.

### What is the moon calendar?

The moon calendar shows the lunar phases and the biodynamic days (root, leaf, flower, fruit) to help you choose the best times to sow, plant, prune and harvest.

### What are biodynamic days?

Biodynamic days sort time into four types (root, leaf, flower and fruit) according to the position of the moon in the zodiac. Each type of day suits certain gardening tasks.

### Does Jeannot give weather forecasts?

Yes. Jeannot gives daily and hourly forecasts for your garden, and alerts to help you prepare for rain, frost, storms or heatwaves.

### How do tasks work?

Jeannot suggests the tasks that follow from the plants in your garden. You can plan each one on a day, with the recommended days of the moon calendar to guide you, and be reminded when the day comes.

### What can the assistant do?

Ask Jeannot anything about plants, gardening techniques or biodynamic practices, and it answers with your garden in mind. It can also identify a plant or a disease from a photo and show you where to prune a tree. Its answers are generated by artificial intelligence and can be wrong: check what matters, and never eat a plant because Jeannot named it.

## Settings

### Can I change the weather units?

Yes. You can choose between Celsius and Fahrenheit for temperatures and between metric and imperial units for measurements.

### Why do I need to give the location of my garden?

Your location is used to calculate the moon phases and the biodynamic days precisely, and to give you the local weather forecast.

## Account and subscription

### Do I need an account to use Jeannot?

Yes. An account keeps your garden, your preferences and your tasks, and lets you find them on all your devices and on the web version.

### Is Jeannot free?

Part of Jeannot is free, such as drawing your garden plan and the moon information of the day. Other features, such as the assistant, planned tasks, the full calendar and the advice on your plan, need a Jeannot Premium subscription. The app shows what Premium includes, and its price, before you subscribe.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

In the subscription settings of your Apple or Google account, at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You keep Premium until the end of the period already paid. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

### How do I delete my account?

In the app, open the settings, then your account, and choose to delete it. This is permanent. Cancel any running subscription in the store first.

## Troubleshooting

### My moon calendar does not match other calendars I have seen. Why?

Moon calendars can differ with the calculation method and the place. Jeannot uses precise astronomical data, calculated for the position of your garden.

### The assistant gave me an unclear answer. What should I do?

Try asking your question another way, with more detail about your plant and your garden. For a precise technique, the encyclopedia and the moon calendar are also there.

### How do I update the app?

Updates are published on the App Store (iPhone, iPad) and on Google Play (Android). Keep Jeannot up to date to get the latest features and fixes. The web version is always up to date.
